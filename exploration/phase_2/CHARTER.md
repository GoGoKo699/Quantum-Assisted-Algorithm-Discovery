# Phase 2: simple mechanisms for consequential quantum advantage

Author decision: 25 September 2026. Target journal: **PRX Quantum**. Manuscript preparation remains **on hold**. There is no submission deadline or requirement to produce a theorem at every checkpoint.

## What is being reset

This is a new exploration phase, not an attempt to make the current classical extraction theorem look quantum. The earlier e-graph, arithmetic-synthesis, and sorting investigations remain available as evidence and tools. Their accumulated machinery does not determine the next question. Earlier publication plans that confine exploration to e-graphs or nominate another journal do not govern this phase.

The leading organizing idea remains quantum-assisted discovery of a reusable classical method: a quantum computation produces a finite object that ordinary computers subsequently use. This is a hypothesis worth pursuing, not an advantage already established. A change to that objective or to the input/access model must be stated explicitly, not smuggled into an example.

The original circuit-model setting remains. Fault-tolerant computation can be assumed while accounting for logical resources; a hardware demonstration is not a prerequisite. Small discovery hardware is an attractive possibility, not a promised resource bound. No commitment to a device platform, QML, photonics, or a specific application is inferred. Error/residual correction is not the chosen direction.

## What simple means

The central mechanism should admit a short explanation containing three things: what becomes possible, why quantum processing changes the situation, and what useful consequence survives the complete comparison. The proof may be difficult. Simplicity does not mean deleting a costly prerequisite, choosing an artificially weak classical model, or disguising many assumptions as one oracle.

A compact finite example should expose the mechanism before large infrastructure is built. That example is an explanation or falsification device, not evidence of novelty or useful advantage by itself.

We do not require an entirely new quantum primitive or an exponential speedup. An existing primitive can enable a new consequential capability. Conversely, a standard square-root upper bound over a chosen enumerator is not enough without the application-specific reason it matters.

## Opening distinction

Candidate testing asks whether a proposed construction is good. Structure discovery asks for information from which a good construction follows. The first route remains legitimate, but it is no longer our default.

The initial question is:

> Can quantum processing reveal a compact structural relation that directly enables a reusable classical shortcut, rather than merely checking more possible shortcuts?

Possible relations include a useful decomposition, a change of representation, or a compositional rule. These are placeholders for investigation, not claims that quantum algorithms for them exist. The relation must actually produce a cheaper classical method; identifying an interesting symmetry alone is insufficient.

## Two distinct sources of advantage to examine

**Structure exposed by coherent computation.** Both designers start from the same explicit classical specification. The quantum computation extracts a relation through interference or another genuinely quantum operation; a short classical construction then uses it. Any coherent access routine, state preparation, normalization, and sampling must be implemented from that specification. A black-box separation alone does not settle the explicit-input task. Standard algebra, classical randomized learning, and representation changes must be permitted.

**Quantum preparation of reusable classical information.** The difficult step creates a finite classical description rather than a faster per-query quantum service. Preparation and use may have different complexity. The key comparison includes the best classical way to obtain an equally useful object or bypass it entirely. An exponentially long answer table is not a reusable algorithm. If physical quantum examples or an oracle are supplied, that is a distinct access model and needs separate justification.

These overlap conceptually but need different evidence. Neither is restricted to machine learning. No working quantum mechanism or application is asserted at this restart.

## The comparison that cannot be omitted

For a specification D and R later uses, account for quantum discovery, certification, and R classical executions. Compare against the best legitimate classical strategy for the same required outputs, not just a classical imitation of the quantum designer. That competitor may discover another method, exploit an existing construction, or answer the R tasks without compiling any method.

Reuse can make an expensive discovery worthwhile; it does not uniquely favor a quantum discovery. A finite description can be inexpensive to execute and still expensive to find, but that gap must be established for the actual task.

Do not require easy discovery and easy checking simultaneously by assumption. Correctness can be certified algebraically, checked computationally, or supported by an explicitly probabilistic guarantee, but its cost and meaning must be included.

## Working rhythm

Develop a few short mechanism sketches. For each, record the useful consequence, the quantum-specific step, the strongest obvious classical response, and the most likely hidden cost. Pursue the most discriminating calculation or primary-source check next. Do not demand that one project resolve every concern about quantum computing.

Do not produce a new solver, exhaustive census, or circuit compiler merely because it is feasible. Code should decide a question about the proposed mechanism. A failed candidate can remain a short note; not every rejection needs its own framework. Repeated discovery of easy cases is not a publishable general negative result.

The manuscript stays on hold until there is a central result worth explaining. No venue substitution, manuscript revival, submission, outside contact, paid computation, or unattended work follows from this charter. Prior research branches and their source/results remain intact.
