Note 04 retains its coherent input/RAM assumptions. Its mathematical audit and
publication priority are unresolved; a smaller random seed is not a QRAM-free
algorithm. Graphs are not universally excluded, but a new eligible construction
must expose all access costs rather than inherit the old time bound.

The next task is useful-model screening under that constraint, not a memory-device
project or a forced QROM retrofit. Sensing remains scope-separated, stopping power
a reserve, and the phase-3 emitter closeout stays in force. No new repository,
platform commitment or manuscript follows. All prior evidence is preserved.
