# Current status

29 September 2026 · **Repository aligned after the no-QRAM decision.**

**Allowed:** fault-tolerant gate-model processors with explicitly costed circuits.
**Excluded:** assumed fast QRAM for input or working tables.
No useful end-to-end quantum advantage is established. Manuscript preparation is on hold.

## Current disposition

The [hardware contract](AGENTS.md) and
[decision 05](exploration/phase_4/NO_QRAM_HARDWARE_BOUNDARY_05.md) are binding.
Reversible formulas and compiled lookups remain possible only with their actual
costs. No forecast of QRAM impossibility or assertion of present hardware capability
is implied by this project boundary.

[Short-seed sparsification 04](exploration/phase_4/SHORT_SEED_SPARSIFICATION_04.md)
is retained as conditional coherent-RAM theory and **parked as an active advantage
lead**. It reduces randomness storage but not every coherent data access. Its
proof audit and publication priority are unresolved; the hardware decision is
neither a mathematical retraction nor permission for an automatic QROM retrofit.

No replacement candidate is selected. The
[current work order](work_orders/CURRENT.md) governs future circuit-model screening;
it does not initiate research during this cleanup. The earlier emitter-covariance
closeout remains in force. Sensing is scope-separated, and stopping power is a
reserve, not an approved or parallel program. Both classical spin-offs are independent.
