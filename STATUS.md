**Fault-tolerant gate-model processors are permitted; fast QRAM is not an allowed
resource assumption. No useful end-to-end quantum advantage is established.
Manuscript preparation remains on hold.**

[Decision 05](exploration/phase_4/NO_QRAM_HARDWARE_BOUNDARY_05.md) and

fault-tolerant processors are available today.
