# Current status

30 September 2026 — source-grounded risk-aware pricing screen.

**Conditional investigation, not a useful quantum advantage. No assumed QRAM.
Manuscript preparation remains on hold.**

[Note 08](exploration/phase_4/RISK_AWARE_PRICING_GATE_08.md) identifies an independently
motivated extension: offline allocation of uncertain cloud workloads with capacity
A(x)+sqrt(q S(x))<=C. Independent-Gaussian, moment-bound and bounded-support risk
models have distinct assumptions. Estimated means, a diagonal covariance, and a
snapshot risk guarantee do not establish an all-server/all-time service guarantee.
The new risk constraint is not covered by Note 07's one-counter DP or equal-size
aggregation; two jobs with the same mean can have different uncertainty.

A sign-guarded squared comparison supplies an ordinary-circuit feasibility test.
Sequential constants, reversible sums/products, and retained selection bits give
an explicit feasible-prefix construction. Its symbolic amplification budget is
conditional on the unknown marked mass. Precision, product width, setup, routing,
model estimation and output validation remain charged. No risk-sampling oracle,
QRAM or freely updated coherent work table is assumed.

The useful marking threshold is drawn from the published hybrid strategy: a feasible
score v>1 and v>=z_R/L implies that exact pricing at that dual cannot improve the
current Farley bound L. This is prior optimization theory, not a new result. The
quantum routine still does not certify absence, global optimality, or faster master
convergence. Bounds on the current master and numerical duals require validation.

Published DW-Hybrid statistics report pricing-time shares 96%, 73%, and 8% for
100-, 400-, and 1000-job generated families, respectively. They are source-reported
shifted-geometric aggregates, not our measured runtime or a quantum-substitutable
fraction. The full branch-and-price experiment includes tasks outside a root-only
circuit. No speedup is inferred by dividing these statistics or enlarging n.

Stronger later classical bounds remain decisive. Kim and Lee's non-convex relaxation,
feasible approximations, chance-constrained PTAS methods, robust/pseudopolynomial
methods, and native hybrid-pricing heuristics are allowed. Quantum chance-constrained
knapsack has existing 2026 literature; its latest version reports comparable
solution quality, not an established speedup for this cloud-pricing interface.

The [work order](work_orders/CURRENT.md) selects one threshold-aware residual-call
test in the source-motivated medium family. Ordinary deterministic one-dimensional
pricing is no longer the default test path; Notes 06-07 remain intact. No new cloud
solver, arbitrary added correlations, extreme precision, or larger benchmark campaign
is selected. A cheap classical witness or sufficient gap ends the quantum call.
