# Publication target — exploration phase 2

Author decision, 25 September 2026: **PRX Quantum** is the target journal. Manuscript preparation remains **on hold**. No deadline is imposed.

The scientific target is a simple central quantum mechanism with a consequential implication for useful computing, particularly discovery of reusable classical methods. The journal choice does not establish significance or acceptance prospects. A difficult proof is compatible with a simple idea; elaborate bookkeeping is not a substitute for that idea.

The earlier extraction manuscript and candidate theorems are preserved as prior work-in-progress. Do not recast a classical result as quantum by appending a generic search corollary, and do not automatically retarget it to a classical journal in this phase. No new quantum primitive, exponential speedup, hardware demonstration, or world-record identity is required by fiat.

The governing research instructions are [the phase-2 charter](../exploration/phase_2/CHARTER.md) and [the current work order](../work_orders/CURRENT.md). These supersede the previous e-graph-only exploration restriction and venue preference for this branch. The old plan remains in the unchanged parent history and on the prior branch.
