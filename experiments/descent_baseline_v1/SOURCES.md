[1] Andrew V. Sutherland, *A Generic Approach to Searching for Jacobians*,
Mathematics of Computation 78 (2009), 485-507, DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-08-02143-1.

and #J_2=P(1)P(-1). Its introduction and quotient-group discussion also describe
using the base curve and twist instead of an undifferentiated extension group.
The source is not a directly executed quantum implementation of our precise
endpoint schedule. The Lemma 3 page screenshot failed; the parsed formula was
read. The final AMS PDF could not be obtained. We do not rely on the preprint's
argument that the natural subgroups intersect trivially: the order identity
follows directly from the roots, and Note 27 explicitly retains rational
2-torsion. No claim about corrections in other versions is made.

https://math.mit.edu/~drew/Definitions.html

[2] Kiran S. Kedlaya, *The relative class number one problem for function fields,
I*, Research in Number Theory (2022), DOI 10.1007/s40993-022-00369-y.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40993-022-00369-y

No numerical tables or figures are used.
