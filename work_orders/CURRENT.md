## Completed small-workload screen

All eleven published coefficient banks are resolved for unweighted binary-node count versus arithmetic depth within the explicit bounded positive-odd shifted-add model. Depth-two synthesis reduces to choosing first-layer helpers of form 2^s +/- 1 and a monotone coverage problem. Three banks cannot have depth two by the established signed-digit bound; exact depth-aware helper search handles their relevant fronts. The largest bank has 25 nodes at minimum depth three with no non-output helper. The full screen took roughly 0.27 seconds in one local run. Timing excludes imports and independent verification and is not a portable or native-solver comparison.

The complete Pareto points and all exact certificates are regenerated by the new scripts. The eight depth-two feasible banks require at most eleven simultaneous nondominated helper portfolios in the tested coverage recurrence. The new result is constructive classical easiness of these finite tasks, not a new circuit record, general efficient MCM algorithm, mapped hardware speedup, or quantum advantage.
