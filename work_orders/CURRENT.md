## Immediate bounded check: an experimentally relevant NMR spectrum

Identify one experimentally studied nuclear-spin system and a useful, resolution-
limited spectral inference or prediction task. Trace the supplied spin Hamiltonian,
field/pulse conditions, measured spectrum and strongest adequate classical
calculation. NMR parameter acquisition from electronic chemistry is a separate cost;
a quantum spin-response calculation does not automatically accelerate that task.

Direct many-body spectral sampling and quantum-assisted NMR inference are prior
work. The proposed output law and a high-temperature operator-state preparation
are specified in Note 14. These are standard identities, not new primitives.
Finite-resolution phase estimation is not an exact frequency measurement, and its
broadening must not be silently identified with instrument broadening. Absolute
intensity, normalized correlation shape and signed multi-pulse signals differ.
The high-temperature nuclear-spin approximation must be physically justified;
arbitrary ground/thermal-state preparation is not granted for other problems.

Check classical restricted-state, tensor-network, spectral-library and direct
fitting routes before attributing difficulty to spin count. Some protein-scale
NMR experiments are already classically tractable. The harmonic vibronic-spectrum
counterexample in Note 14 shows that useful aggregated spectra can be classically
accessible even when the associated microscopic sampling problem looks hard.

Determine whether the correlations and time resolution needed for the actual
experimental distinction expose a nontrivial sampling opportunity. Compare at
that useful accuracy, including parameter inputs, state preparation, controlled
and conjugate evolution, shots, fitting repetitions, noise, relaxation and
validation. Neither a routine small-molecule replay nor an unnecessary exact
line list is an application result. A validated limited model is allowed; no
new native NMR package, general simulator, large training run or broad census
is requested. No specific compound or positive quantum margin is selected yet.
