# Project map

29 September 2026 · **One parent exploration; two independent spin-offs.**

## Objective and current boundary

Obtain useful information with a fault-tolerant gate-model processor more effectively
than strong classical alternatives. Direct samples and reusable classical artifacts
are both allowed. Model-first analysis retains preparation, accuracy, output, reuse,
and ALL input/working-data costs.

**No assumed fast QRAM.** Coherent logical computation is allowed; arbitrary large
classical tables do not receive free access in superposition. Reversible formulas
and compiled lookups must be charged as circuits, including updates and workspace.
The binding [working rules](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Assisted-Algorithm-Discovery/blob/research/prx-quantum-phase2/AGENTS.md) and
[decision 05](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Assisted-Algorithm-Discovery/blob/research/prx-quantum-phase2/exploration/phase_4/NO_QRAM_HARDWARE_BOUNDARY_05.md)
supersede older permission to pursue QRAM-conditional advantage leads.

The short-seed sparsifier is parked under this hardware model, not mathematically
retracted. The activity-covariance lead remains closed on current evidence. No
replacement candidate or new phase is selected by this maintenance. No useful
end-to-end advantage is established; manuscript preparation remains on hold.
